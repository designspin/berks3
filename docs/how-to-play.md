# How to Play

## Objective

Using your quick-firing terror tank, you must penetrate the city of the Berks and collect **21 keys**, all heavily entombed in building blocks.

Once collected, you gain access to the **Inner Sanctum**. Blast your way to 4 more keys to enter the Heart of the Inner Sanctum.

Inside, collect **10 more keys** to gain entry to the **Berks' Sacred Treasure Chamber**. Recover the treasure to complete your mission!

## Enemies

- **Berks** - The main enemies hunting you throughout the city
- **Drones** - Additional threats with different movement patterns

## Power-ups

- **Hearts** - Collect to gain extra lives (maximum 9 lives)
- **Keys** - Required to progress through the game

## Tips

- You start with 5 lives
- Destructible blocks can be shot through to create paths
- Even the protective blocks will destroy you on contact - be careful!
