# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: January 2026**

## Overview

Berks 3 ("the App") is developed by Jason Foster. This privacy policy explains how we handle your information.

## Data Collection

### What We Collect

**Berks 3 does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

The App may use the following platform services:

- **Apple Game Center** - If you choose to sign in, Game Center handles your leaderboard scores and achievements. This data is managed by Apple according to their [Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

### What We Don't Collect

- No analytics or tracking
- No advertising
- No personal information
- No usage data

## Third-Party Services

The only third-party service used is Apple Game Center, which is optional. We have no access to your Game Center data - it is managed entirely by Apple.

## Children's Privacy

Berks 3 does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
